## Added

- `cfdbender.core.farfield`, the numeric core of the far-field family on plain arrays, with no input or output and no solver import. `LatticeFields` and `PlaneFields` hold the samples of an annular survey lattice and of a rectangular plane; `lattice_fields`, `plane_fields` and `cylindrical_components` build them. The quadrature layer (`plane_integral`, `ring_sample_weights`, `grid_integral`, `grid_weights`, `grid_coverage`, `sample_coverage`, `sample_area_coverage`, `strip_edges`, `azimuthal_harmonics`, `symmetry_floor`) propagates a missing sample as NaN and never drops it. The balances (`mass_flux`, `mass_closure`, `axial_flux`, `axial_force`, `transverse_flux`, `transverse_force`, `lateral_flux`, `shaft_torque`, `in_plane_moment`, `crossflow_kinetic_energy`, `crossflow_integrand`, `to_counts`, `spurious_diagnostic`) take the density as an argument, and every ledger is in the field's units times the square of the length unit of `tip_radius`. A refusal is a `FarfieldInputError`, a `ValueError`. Nothing in the matrix, the plan or a solver adapter calls it yet.
- `cfdbender.core.farfield` also holds the Trefftz numerics. `trace_forces` and `trace_coefficients` give the lift, side force and induced drag of a discrete trailing-vortex trace of section loads (strip edges, normalwash, family shares, mirror rule); `plane_induced_drag` (crossflow energy), `plane_vorticity_drag` (stream function) and `plane_lift` work on a rectangular plane of velocities; `bernoulli_residual` is the Bernoulli field check of that plane, a diagnostic that is never a drag; `rotor_channels` gives the axial, swirl and crossflow power channels and the far-field efficiency at one lattice station, with the free-stream density. The vortex-wake methods and the disk total-pressure rise are not part of it.
